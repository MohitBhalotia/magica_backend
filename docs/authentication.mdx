---
title: "Authentication"
description: "API keys, and what a key can and can't do."
---

Send your key as a bearer token on every request:

```http
Authorization: Bearer ak_3f9Q2x…
```

- **Where keys come from:** you create them in the app (user menu → **API keys**). Each key is shown once; we store only its SHA-256 hash.
- **What a key can do:** act as you on every endpoint, for your own chats, runs, credits and webhooks only. Anything that isn't yours answers `404`, the same as something that doesn't exist.
- **What a key can't do:** create, list or revoke keys. That needs the signed-in app, so a leaked key can't mint more.
- **Revoking:** revoke a key in the same dialog. It stops working at once.
- **Errors:** an unknown or revoked key answers `401 UNAUTHENTICATED`.

<Warning>Keep keys on your server. Never put them in a browser or mobile app.</Warning>
